Getting the offer feels like the finish line, but there are still five steps before you're sitting in a lecture. None of them are hard. Most of the risk is in missing a deadline.

## 1. Accept your offer

Pay the enrolment fee by the deadline in your acceptance letter. **Missing it can cancel the offer**, and universities rarely make exceptions. If you're waiting on a scholarship decision, ask the university about the deadline early rather than late.

## 2. Your university applies for your Certificate of Eligibility

The Certificate of Eligibility, 在留資格認定証明書 or **COE**, is what lets you apply for a student visa. You don't apply for it yourself. **The university applies to immigration for you**, using documents you send them: usually your passport copy, photos and proof of funds.

Send what they ask for quickly and exactly. A missing document here is the most common cause of delay. The COE can now be issued electronically, which saves postage time.

## 3. Get your student visa

Take your COE and passport to a Japanese embassy or consulate in your country. For Myanmar, that's the Embassy of Japan in Yangon. Check its current procedure and processing time before you book anything.

## 4. Arrive in Japan

At the main airports (Narita, Haneda, Kansai, Chubu and a few others) you receive your **residence card, 在留カード**, on arrival. Keep it with you; you need it for almost everything.

You can also apply for **permission to work part-time** at the airport. It's the easiest moment to do it, so don't skip it.

## 5. Register where you live

Within **14 days** of moving in, register your address at your city or ward office. At the same visit, join **National Health Insurance, 国民健康保険**. It covers 70% of most medical costs, and it's required.

After that you can open a bank account and get a phone contract, which usually need both your residence card and a registered address.

## A quick checklist

- [ ] Enrolment fee paid before the deadline
- [ ] COE documents sent to the university
- [ ] Visa issued
- [ ] Residence card received at the airport
- [ ] Work permission applied for
- [ ] Address registered within 14 days
- [ ] National Health Insurance joined
