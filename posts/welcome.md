Hello, I'm Eric. I teach EJU maths in Osaka, in Burmese, and I built this site so that students from Myanmar have one place for the whole road from the EJU to a Japanese university.

This blog is the part of the site where I write in my own voice: short posts on EJU maths, on choosing a university, and on what daily life in Japan is really like. Most posts will be in Burmese. Some will be in English or Japanese, and the Blog page lets you show one language at a time.

## What is on the rest of the site

- Every EJU formula for maths, physics and chemistry, with diagrams, flashcards and quizzes.
- Every university in Japan, with filters for how each one uses the EJU, and a list you can save.
- 104 scholarships that a student from Myanmar can apply for, each linked to its official page.
- The documents, the statement of purpose, the interview and the essay, step by step.

If you sign in with Google, the things you save follow you from your phone to your laptop.

## A note on maths in posts

Formulas work here too. The quadratic formula, for example, is

$$x = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a}$$

and inline maths like $\sin^2\theta + \cos^2\theta = 1$ sits inside a sentence.
