Every university has its own list in its 募集要項 (application guidelines), and that list is the final word. But most lists look very similar. Here's the typical set, grouped the way you'll collect it, with the details that most often get an application returned.

You can tick these off as you go in [Study → Application](#/study/apply). Your ticks are saved in your browser, or in your account if you sign in with Google.

## Check this first: 12 years of schooling

Japanese universities require **12 years of schooling** before university. If your school system had 11 years, you'll need a designated preparatory course first. Check this before you spend time on anything else.

## Identity and the form

- **Passport**, a copy of the photo page. Make sure it doesn't expire during your application period.
- **Residence card (在留カード)**, both sides, only if you're already in Japan.
- **EJU examinee number.** Universities look up your score through JASSO with it, so copy it exactly.
- **Application form (願書).** Comes with the 募集要項. Black ink, no correction fluid.
- **Photos.** Size and rules are set by each university.

## From your school

- **Academic transcript (成績証明書).** Often it must arrive in an envelope sealed by your school.
- **Graduation certificate (卒業証明書).** A separate document from the transcript.
- **Certified translation** into Japanese or English, if the originals are in another language. The translator's name and stamp are usually required.

## Language evidence

- **JLPT certificate**, if you have one. Some faculties require N2 alongside the EJU.
- **Japanese language school attendance record.** Many universities ask for it, and low attendance can also affect your visa renewal.

## What you write

- **Statement of purpose (志望理由書).** Some universities want it handwritten, so check before you type it. I've written a separate post on [how to structure it](#/blog/statement-of-purpose).
- **Letter of recommendation (推薦書).** Ask your teacher at least three weeks ahead.

## Money

- **Proof of funds** or a bank balance certificate. Often a specific minimum, named in the 募集要項.
- **Your supporter's employment or income certificate**, if someone else is funding you.

## Sending it

- **Application fee (検定料) receipt.** Keep a copy for yourself too.
- **Addressed reply envelope.** Cross out 行 and write 御中 on envelopes addressed to the university.
- **Send by registered mail (簡易書留)** and keep the receipt until the university confirms it arrived.

## Timing

Transcripts, certificates and translations take the longest, especially from Myanmar. Request them as soon as you've decided where to apply, even before you've finished your statement.
