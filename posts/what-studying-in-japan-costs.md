Money is usually the first real question, so here are the numbers. Each university sets its own fees, so treat these as the scale of things and check the 募集要項 for the real figure.

## Tuition

**National universities** charge a standard rate of **¥535,800 a year**, plus **¥282,000** when you enrol. A university may charge up to 20% more, up to ¥642,960 a year. The University of Tokyo does this for students starting in 2025 or later.

**Public universities** (run by a prefecture or city) are close to the national rates. Many charge a higher enrolment fee to students from outside their prefecture or city, so check which rate applies to you.

**Private universities** vary a lot by faculty. For students who started in 2025, the averages were:

| | Average |
|---|---|
| Tuition | ¥968,069 a year |
| Enrolment fee | ¥240,365 |
| Facilities fee | ¥172,550 a year |
| First year, with other fees | ¥1,507,647 |

Medicine, dentistry and art schools are well above these averages. Humanities faculties are usually below.

## Living costs

Self-funded international students spent **about ¥170,000 a month** on average, including their school costs. Rent averaged **¥41,000 a month**, and **¥51,000** in the Kanto region, the most expensive. That survey ran from November 2023 to March 2024.

Outside Tokyo, you can usually live for noticeably less, which is one reason regional national universities are worth a serious look.

## Working while you study

You need permission first: **資格外活動許可** (permission to engage in activity other than that permitted). With it, you can work:

- up to **28 hours a week** during term, and
- up to **8 hours a day** during your school's long holidays.

New students can apply at the airport when they first arrive, or later at an immigration office. Working over the limit can put your visa at risk, so take the 28 hours seriously.

## Scholarships

Most students combine savings, family support, part-time work and a scholarship. There are 104 scholarships open to students from Myanmar on the [Scholarships page](#/scholarships), each linked to its official page, plus a quick check against the JASSO criteria.
